Real plates move about 5 cm per year. Add foam plates to compare your model.

Procedure

  1. Hands
    Press Start. The heat is on under the left end and the ice cube is in the right end.
  2. Hands
    Press Drop red dye. It lands on the bottom, right over the heat.
  3. Recorder
    The tank takes pictures at 1, 4 and 8 minutes. Sketch each one on your handout. Use red arrows for warm water and blue arrows for cold water.
  4. Hands
    Press Add foam plates. Record which way they drift.
  5. Reporter
    Write the claim: "The red water went ___ because ___. The blue water went ___ because ___."
