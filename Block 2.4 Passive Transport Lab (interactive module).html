Room temperature 22 °C. Each core is about 1 cm wide and 3 cm long. Your station number changes which potato you get, so stations will not match exactly.

The Lethal IV case

Patient file. A 67-year-old man is admitted for dehydration. The order reads: “1,000 mL 0.9% sodium chloride IV.” About an hour after the new bag is hung, his urine turns dark red-brown, his back hurts and his blood pressure drops. A nurse stops the IV.

Four 1,000 mL bags were in the supply drawer at his bedside. The lab adds a drop of a healthy person’s blood to fluid from each bag and watches the red blood cells. Your job: test all four bags, find the one that was hung, and explain what it did to his cells.
